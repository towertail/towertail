This release fixes one bug and makes the app do less work.

## Fixed

- **SSH port in the terminal.** The terminal button on a card and "Open SSH" in Preferences now use the SSH port of the host. Before, they always connected to port 22.

## Other changes

- The full view and the log pane do less work when no new data comes.
